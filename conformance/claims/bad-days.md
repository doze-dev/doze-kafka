# Claims: Bad days (stage 1, "Durability first")

The recording queue of `plan/stages/bad-days.md` section 8, as far as stage 1 got.
Q2 (every write API on a full volume), Q5, Q9 (acks=0), Q10 and Q11 (EndTxn on a decided transaction) (what a compressed batch is held to) are recorded against `apache/kafka:4.3.1`; the rest stay at `memory` or
`doc` until a stage that needs them records them.
