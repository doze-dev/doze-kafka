The same ACL from a terminal 
kafka-acls.sh --bootstrap-server 127.0.0.1:9092 --command-config admin.properties --add \
  --allow-principal User:validator --allow-host '*' \
  --operation Read --topic harbour.checkout.orders.retry
Read also lets the principal Describe the topic. For a prefix, add --resource-pattern-type prefixed and give the prefix as the name.
What this will doThe topic harbour.checkout.orders.retry has no ACL yet and is open to everyone. After this, only User:validator can read it; every other client is refused with TOPIC_AUTHORIZATION_FAILED, including the one that writes it, until you add a Write ACL.
